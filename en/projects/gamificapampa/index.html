The idea

GamificaPampa brings together outreach projects from UNIPAMPA (Alegrete campus) that take artificial intelligence and gamification to public-school teachers in Alegrete, Brazil: Educador 5.0, on AI, and GamificaEdu, on gamification. The site treats teacher training as a game in three levels: Discover, Create and Apply.

My part is what ends up in the hands of teachers and kids. I started with games that introduce programming and the Software Engineering degree; then came games for other subjects, made as examples of what a teacher can build after the Workshop.

What I made

Six programming games

Bug Ninja, Pair Programming, Connect the System, Code Arcade, Bug Hunt and Dev Mission. Kids hunt bugs, program in pairs (one drives, the other navigates) and wire a phone to a server and a database.

Thirteen subject games

From first grade to high school: Syllable Factory, Way to School, Colour Studio, Bit's Circuit, Fractions Pizzeria, Water Cycle, Timeline, English with Bit, Bit's Scale, News Detective, Bit's Line, Equation Lab and Bit's Genetics.

For teachers

  • Lesson with the games: 50 minutes on how a program gets made, for classes aged 6 to 14, with script, slides, worksheet and certificate.
  • Prompt Workshop: four sessions on asking Gemini for lesson plans, activities, assessments and games, with the Prompt Game, a prompt builder and an online course for those who can't attend.
  • Prompt Machine: the teacher picks the class, subject, topic and what to make, and copies a ready prompt for Gemini.
  • Offline kit and printable medals.

How it was designed

QR codes and short links

The teacher projects the class menu and kids open the game on their phones through a QR code. Every resource has a short link to write on the board and a button to send it over WhatsApp or Classroom.

No sign-up

No game asks for a login, and the site collects no data from kids. Open it and play, on a phone, a tablet or a projector.

When the network drops

The offline kit packs the class menu, the games and the lesson into one file that opens with a double click on the school laptop. A GitHub workflow rebuilds it on every change to the repository.

Ready to print

Script, worksheet, certificates and medals print on A4, straight from the pages themselves.

From a personal project to the organization

The games and the Workshop started in a repository of mine, Código Mirim. When they became part of GamificaPampa, they moved to the GitHub organization: what teachers and kids use stayed public, and the facilitators' kit went to a private team repository.

The rule of the move was not to break a single address. There were already QR codes printed on booths, slides and invitations, so every old address got a new destination, and the old repository became a bridge that sends each link to the right place.

In schools and at events

On October 14, 2026, GamificaPampa was presented to the teachers and management team of Escola Tancredo Neves, in Alegrete, and to the Diálogos do Pampa research group at UNIPAMPA's São Borja campus, in the talk "AI in Education".
