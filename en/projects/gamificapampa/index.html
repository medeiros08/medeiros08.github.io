The idea

GamificaPampa brings together outreach projects from UNIPAMPA (Alegrete campus) that take artificial intelligence and gamification to public-school teachers in Alegrete, Brazil: Educador 5.0, on AI, and GamificaEdu, on gamification. The site treats teacher training as a game in three levels: Discover, Create and Apply.

My part is what ends up in the hands of teachers and kids. I started with games that introduce programming and the Software Engineering degree; then came games for other subjects, made as examples of what a teacher can build after the Prompt Workshop.

How it was designed

  1. A QR code for the class

    The teacher projects the class menu and kids open the games on their phones through a QR code. Every resource has a short link to write on the board and a Share button that sends it over WhatsApp or Classroom.

  2. When the network drops

    The offline kit packs the class menu, the games and the lesson into one file that opens with a double click on the school laptop. A GitHub workflow rebuilds it on every change.

  3. No sign-up

    No game asks for a login, and the site collects no data from kids. Open it and play, on a phone, a tablet or a projector.

  4. Ready to print

    Script, worksheet, certificates and medals print on A4, straight from the pages themselves.

Inside

From a personal project to the organization

The games and the Workshop started in a repository of mine, Código Mirim. When they became part of GamificaPampa, they moved to the GamificaPampa organization on GitHub: what teachers and kids use stayed public, and the facilitators' kit went to a private team repository.

The rule of the move was not to break a single address. There were already QR codes printed on booths, slides and invitations, so every old address got a new destination, and the old repository became a bridge that sends each link to the right place.

In schools and at events

On October 14, 2026, GamificaPampa was presented to the teachers and management team of Escola Tancredo Neves, in Alegrete, and to the Diálogos do Pampa research group at UNIPAMPA's São Borja campus, in the talk "AI in Education".
